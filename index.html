Where is the chamber, and when does the doctor see patients?

At Lakeview Diagnostic Centre, Road 27, Dhanmondi, Dhaka. Saturday to Thursday from 5 PM to 9 PM, and Friday from 10 AM to 1 PM.

How do I get a serial or an appointment?

Call 01700-000000 for a serial, or send a request through the appointment form on this website. If you send a request online, the chamber will call you to confirm a time.

Is my appointment confirmed as soon as I send an online request?

No. An online request is only a request. Your appointment is confirmed once the chamber calls you with your serial and time.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Bring all previous prescriptions, recent test reports and a list, or the packets, of the medicines you take. If you have diabetes, your home blood sugar notes are helpful too.

When should I see an endocrinologist?

Endocrinologists treat diabetes, thyroid problems, PCOS, obesity, bone loss and vitamin D deficiency, as well as diseases of hormone glands such as the adrenal and pituitary glands. Your GP may also refer you.

Do I need to fast before hormone tests?

Not always. Some tests need fasting and others must be done at a particular time of day. Follow the instructions from your doctor or the laboratory for each test.
